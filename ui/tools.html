Her hands

Give her agents and tools — Hermes Agent, nanoMuse, smart-home control, anything that speaks MCP. Enable one and she knows she has it. She decides whether to use it; nothing runs until you asked and she agreed.

How she uses them道具
She speaks first, as always. After she has answered, a small metered clerk call reads what you asked and what she said out loud, and hands over only what she agreed to — quoting your own words as proof. She can refuse. She can't start anything on her own.
Agents report back through her: progress, questions, approval requests, the final result. An agent's approval is released only by your yes in conversation or a click on the Use page — never by her.

Smart Home and IoT checking…

Safety first. Amika is an AI: she can mishear, misunderstand, or switch the wrong device at the wrong time, and nothing guarantees that she will not cause harm. Everything you connect is at your own risk. Connect only things that are harmless if switched by mistake: never heaters, cooking appliances, locks, valves, alarms, medical equipment or anything else that could cause danger.

Add a server

Recipes — Hermes Agent: python -m openamika_agents hermes (needs hermes gateway with its API server on; put HERMES_API_KEY in the entry's env, see servers/openamika-agents/README.md) · nanoMuse: python -m openamika_agents nanomuse (needs nanomuse serve; NANOMUSE_TOKEN_FILE in env) · Smart home: python -m openamika_iot (cwd ../servers/openamika-iot) · Home Assistant: http → http://homeassistant.local:8123/mcp_server/sse + bearer header · any other MCP server works the same way. Smart-home servers (this one, Home Assistant, or any entry with "kind": "iot") turn on only after you accept the IoT safety statement.
