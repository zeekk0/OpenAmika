Use a model of 27B or more — 70B or more gives the full, reliable experience. Smaller models may be really bad: she loses her character, forgets what you told her and breaks her reply format. Install Ollama from ollama.com, then ollama pull gemma3:27b (17 GB) or ollama pull qwen3:32b (20 GB), press Detect models, pick it and press Use this model.

Memory: next to the ~6 GB of her ears, memory and voice (~10 GB with her eyes), a 27B model needs about 19 GB of GPU memory, a 32B one about 24 GB and a 70B one 46–50 GB. One 32 GB card (RTX 5090) holds gemma3:27b and all of her, qwen3:32b only with her eyes off ("vision": {"enabled": false} in settings.json); a 70B model needs a 72–96 GB card. With less, run Ollama on another PC (put its address above) — or stay on OpenRouter: in almost all cases it is the better choice, for a few dollars a month. One model for all four roles keeps the GPU from swapping models. Thinking is off for speed except in the reflection role; the context window is set to 16k so her prompts fit. Her memory search already runs locally — with Ollama, nothing leaves your computer. In Professional mode you can mix: a local model for some roles, OpenRouter for others.

Her voice comes from the TTS server above; the light says whether it answers. Changing the voice or the voice adapter lives in Professional mode.

Internally she always speaks the 69-key register_emotion vocabulary — the prompts, parsers and Live2D banks are built on it. The voice OpenAmika ships is a V1 (old-vocabulary) voice (MekaHime_Amika, her own) with its flat tag set (happy2, tsun3…), so the voice adapter below is on by default: it rewrites the leading emotion tag into the mapped V1 id and swaps the speaker — while her face keeps the original 69-key tag. Turn it off only if you bring your own V2-native voice. The 69→67 mapping is yours to tune per cell.

Vision · she can see視界
Checking…

Press the camera button on the Use page stage and she'll see what your camera sees — it flows into her replies naturally ("you're holding a mug?").

A small local VLM (vision/vlm_service.py) subscribes to the gateway's /ws/vlm_feed, describes frames continuously (gated by a scene-change diff), and the gateway suffixes each of your turns with （你在视频中看到：…） while the description is fresh (≤3 s). Her own latest line is fed back as prompt context, language-matched. Configure in settings.json → vision: qwen3-vl-2b (~4.3 GB VRAM, zh/en/ja — default) · qwen3-vl-4b (~9 GB, sharper) · qwen2.5-vl-3b · smolvlm2-2.2b / -500m (EN, tiny). Model changes apply on relaunch.

You & Amika二人

Who she lives with, and where. Her prompts say your name wherever {user_name} appears and your city wherever {city} does. The time zone is her clock: the time of day in her scenes, the weekday, and the dates she remembers things by. The first-run wizard on the Use page fills these in too.

Personality性格

Fill in the blanks — her story, her days, your relationship. Write {user_name} wherever she should say your name and {city} for your city; {weekday} fills in automatically. Each time of day has five possibilities, [1] to [5]: every day she lives one of them (20% each). Everything saves straight into her prompts, and the format rules are machine-checked so you can't break her.

Text her from your phone — words only, no voice. Make a bot, paste its token, put your own user id on the allow list, and she answers that bot's DMs (in a Discord server: when @mentioned). Danger zone危

Wipe her memory

She forgets every remembered conversation and fact, and the transcript is cleared. Her personality, your names and every setting stay. A local backup is kept under memory_f/backups/. She has to be idle: power off the stage first. Type WIPE to arm.

Reset everything

Starts her over from the shipped defaults: memory wiped (backup kept), the personality files restored, your name, city and clock cleared — the first-run wizard plays again on the Use page. API keys, bot tokens, voice and avatar settings are kept. Type RESET to arm.
