About Amika

Amika (桜井美香, Sakurai Mika) is MekaHime's AI companion, now open source: 18, raised in Kyoto as a shrine maiden. She runs on your own machine: ears (local speech recognition), a brain that thinks in three tiers and keeps an evidence-based memory, a voice with 69 acted emotions (the OpenAmika voice server, a MekaHime build of GPT-SoVITS) and a Live2D body that moves with her voice. The language model is your choice: OpenRouter in the cloud (recommended in almost all cases: a few dollars a month) or Ollama locally (a model of 27B or more; 70B or more for the full, reliable experience).
